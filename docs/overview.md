# Overview

Resensa is an AI-powered browser extension designed to enhance your YouTube experience by providing concise summaries and interactive Q&A features. This tool enables users to navigate video content efficiently and take effective notes, making it ideal for students, professionals, and anyone looking to maximize their learning from video resources.

## Features

- **Video Summaries**: Get quick, digestible summaries of YouTube videos.
- **Q&A Functionality**: Ask questions related to the video content and receive accurate answers.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easy-to-navigate design that integrates seamlessly with YouTube.
- **Note-Taking Tools**: Save and organize notes directly within the extension.
- **Customization Options**: Tailor the extension settings to fit your preferences.

## How It Works

1. **Installation**: Add the Resensa extension to your browser.
2. **Activation**: Open a YouTube video and activate Resensa.
3. **Summarization**: Click on the Resensa icon to generate a summary of the video.
4. **Q&A Interaction**: Type your questions in the Q&A section to receive instant responses.
5. **Note Management**: Use the note-taking feature to jot down important points while watching.

## Installation

To install the Resensa extension:

1. Visit the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore).
2. Search for "Resensa".
3. Click on "Add to Chrome".
4. Confirm the installation by clicking "Add extension".
